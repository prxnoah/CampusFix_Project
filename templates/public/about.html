About LPU Manila

Founded in 1952 by Dr. José P. Laurel, LPU Manila brings a long tradition of academic excellence into a future-ready, globally connected university experience.

UNIVERSITY PROFILE

Rooted in legacy.
Built for the future.

Lyceum of the Philippines University Manila is a private, non-sectarian institution located within the historic walls of Intramuros. LPU upholds the ideals Veritas et Fortitudo and Pro Deo et Patria while preparing learners for leadership, professional practice and meaningful impact.
