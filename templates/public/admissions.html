ADMISSIONS • AY 2026–2027

Your LPU journey starts here.

Enrollment reservation and admissions processes are ongoing. Check the official LPU Manila admissions page for the latest schedules and requirements.

01

Reservation

For incoming college freshmen, the reservation fee is Php 2,000 and is deductible from the total assessment upon enrollment. Reservation is first-come, first-served.

02

LCET

The LPU College Entrance Test is waived for most programs. BS Accountancy and BS Customs Administration applicants are required to take the entrance examination.

03

Enrollment

Enrollment requirements include the accomplished application and academic/identity documents. Medical clearance is part of the enrollment process.
