Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for the LPU Manila portal and CampusFix maintenance workflow.

01

How do I report a campus issue?

Sign in, open CampusFix, select Report an Issue, identify the building/area, choose severity and submit the concern.

02

Can I track a report?

Yes. Your dashboard shows the current status, priority, assigned personnel and timeline.

03

What if someone already reported it?

CampusFix checks unresolved reports using location and category so maintenance teams can identify potential duplicates.

04

Is CampusFix the official LPU admissions portal?

No. This project integrates a CampusFix maintenance system into an LPU-inspired university portal. Admissions and official university transactions should use LPU's official website.
